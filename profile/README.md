## 🚀 Overview
Ping Reducer for Windows helps your online games feel snappier by optimizing how your connection reaches the game server. It finds a more direct route, prioritizes your game's traffic over background apps, and shows your ping live so you can see the effect. In popular titles like Fortnite and Roblox, a cleaner route and less background interference can mean fewer spikes and steadier play. Built for gamers who want a simple fix for lag, this ping reducer keeps the tuning quick and clear on your PC.

## ✨ Features
- **Route optimization** — Find a more direct path to the game server to help cut latency.
- **Game traffic priority** — Put your game ahead of background downloads and updates.
- **Live ping monitor** — Watch your ping in real time before and after tuning.
- **Game profiles** — Quick presets for popular titles like Fortnite and Roblox.
- **Background cleanup** — Pause bandwidth-hungry apps while you play.
